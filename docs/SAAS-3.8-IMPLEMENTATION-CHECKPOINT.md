This is a historical source and operations checkpoint, not current operating
guidance, deployment identity, a restore attestation, benchmark, invoice or release
closure. Its complete dated source/CI history, provider observations, resource
identities, measurements, owner decisions and execution approvals are retained in
internal release and acceptance evidence.

Current repository `main`, root `AGENTS.md`, engineering standards and permanent
three-customer requirements remain authoritative for implementation. Current
issues, PRs and independently bound deployment/provider evidence own live delivery
status; historical statements that work was open or completed are not a current
status report. Historical failed, cancelled, waived and unexecuted checks retain
their original status. A later success proves only its separately verified scope.

## Independent acceptance boundaries

- Source merge and CI success do not establish a hosted deployment or acceptance
  of a currently served frontend/API pair.
- Isolated fixtures or a provider probe do not establish application authorization,
  coordinated database/object recovery, rollback or real-provider cutover.
- A prepared recovery harness is not an executed restored-pair result.
- Session-read measurements do not establish concurrent booking capacity, cold-start
  behavior, a business-workload SLO or production capacity.
- HTTP body bytes, provider transfer and overlapping product totals are distinct
  measurement boundaries. They must not be added as if they were attributable
  invoice egress. Published allowances and modeled rates are not verified hard caps.
- Offline reports or emitted telemetry do not establish deployed collection,
  delivered alerts, representative workload evidence or complete monthly costs.
